# Group chats into sections

Sections group chats by client or topic without changing their projects. A
section can contain chats from different projects on the same computer.

Choose **New section** in the sidebar. Use a section's menu to rename it,
move it up or down, or delete it. You can also drag section headers to reorder
them. Choose the arrow beside a name to collapse or expand its chats.

Open a chat's sidebar menu and choose **Move to section**, followed by the
section name. **Remove from section** returns the chat to its usual sidebar
position. Each chat belongs to at most one section. Deleting a section never
deletes its chats or changes their projects, histories, approvals or inputs.

Sections are saved on their computer and appear on desktop and in a web
browser, including a phone browser. Each connected computer keeps its own
sections. Older servers do not show section controls.
