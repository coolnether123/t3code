# Celebrate your birthday

In the web or desktop app, open **Settings > Appearance > Birthday celebration**.
Choose your month and day, enable **Celebrate my birthday**, and save. No birth year is needed.

On that date, T3 temporarily changes its palette across the app. Usage and Codex monitor show
a birthday greeting and a cake. Tap **Make a wish** to blow out the candle; tap again to relight it.
Buttons and links release a short confetti burst without changing what the control does.
The layout and charts remain usable on a phone browser.

Open the folded note for a few birthday messages. The starting message and cake colors change
each year. These small surprises stay inside the greeting and do not interrupt your work.

Turn off **Confetti and tap effects** for the birthday colors without animation. The system's
reduced-motion setting also disables the effects. Confetti stops after each tap; nothing runs
continuously while you read. Neither the theme nor the effects change usage measurements.

The celebration follows each device's local calendar date. Your regular theme returns after
your birthday without changing the theme you saved. A February 29 birthday runs on February 29
in leap years. It is not moved to a different day in other years.

Your date stays in your T3 server's private settings, outside the code repository. Paired web
and desktop clients use the same preference. Change it here whenever needed, disable the
celebration, or choose **Remove birthday** to clear the date. The native mobile app does not yet
render this celebration; use the web app in your phone's browser.
